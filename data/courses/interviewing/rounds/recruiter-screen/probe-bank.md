# Recruiter-screen question bank

Recruiter openers. The gym's probes and rubrics here were one person's positioning, so only the questions and generic hints are kept; build your own answers from voice-notes.md.

Source: [ai-engineering-gym](https://github.com/alexeygrigorev/ai-engineering-gym), converted 2026-10-29. Coaching material only —
the gym's sample answers are someone else's career and are deliberately not carried over.
Every answer you rehearse must trace to `jobs/materials/evidence/evidence-store.md`.

## positioning

### What are your salary expectations?

`salary-expectations` · medium

<details><summary>Hints</summary>

- Answer the salary question with a question: get their band before you give a number.
- Lead with 'scope, team, impact first' so declining to anchor reads as senior, not evasive.
- If a form forces a number, use a reasonable senior/staff figure, not extreme, not one you'd reject outright.

</details>

### What are your strengths / what's your edge?

`strengths-and-edge` · medium

<details><summary>Hints</summary>

- Your edge isn't a skill, it's a combination: production ML + product/user empathy + can-explain-it. Say the combination out loud.

</details>

### Tell me about yourself

`tell-me-about-yourself` · easy

<details><summary>Hints</summary>

- Four beats: background -> scale -> what you build now -> why now. Say them in that order and stop.
- Close on 'exploring, being selective': confident, not available-and-anxious.

</details>

### Walk me through your background / current situation

`walk-me-through-your-background` · medium

<details><summary>Hints</summary>

- First words reframe the title: 'Founder and AI/product engineering lead'. Kill the 'community manager' read immediately.

</details>

### What are you looking for?

`what-are-you-looking-for` · easy

<details><summary>Hints</summary>

- Anchor on one primary target (senior/staff applied AI / AI product), then name platform and forward-deployed as adjacent, not a scattershot list.
- The tell that separates you: 'AI as product/workflow infrastructure, not a demo layer.'
- Match the emphasis to their role: lean platform for infra roles, lean forward-deployed for customer-facing ones.

</details>

### Why are you looking / why now?

`why-are-you-looking` · easy

<details><summary>Hints</summary>

- First sentence must be 'not because something is broken': set the frame before any reasons.
- End on your bar (product, team, depth, values) so 'selective' sounds like standards, not indecision.

</details>

### Why this company / why this role?

`why-this-company-role` · hard

<details><summary>Hints</summary>

- One specific, verifiable detail about them first (a product, a problem they're solving) before anything about you.
- Fill this reusable frame per company, and never let it sound like a template. The specificity is the whole point.

</details>
