# MiniGPT-4 Linear Projection Alignment

Zhu et al. (2023) ask whether a single projection layer between a frozen visual encoder and a frozen, more capable LLM (Vicuna) is enough to reproduce demos in the style of GPT-4's vision-language behaviour. Their hypothesis is that GPT-4's multimodal abilities come from using a more advanced LLM. The extraction notes that this claim is not tested directly: the BLIP-2 comparison also changes the LLM and the training recipe.

## Mechanism

- BLIP-2 supplies the EVA-CLIP ViT-G/14 and the Q-Former; a single linear layer joins them to Vicuna and is the only part that is trained.
- Stage 1 uses roughly 5M caption pairs (20,000 steps at batch 256, about 10 hours on 4 A100 80GB). Training on short captions only leads to unnatural output.
- Stage 2 fine-tunes on about 3,500 detailed descriptions, which survive from 5,000 generated by the stage-1 model after ChatGPT repair, rule filtering and manual checking. This stage runs 400 steps at batch size 12 and lasts about seven minutes on one A100 (self-reported).

## Results (self-reported)

- After stage 2, failed detailed-caption generations fall from 35% to 2% and failed poem generations from 32% to 1% (100 random COCO test images each, failures counted by hand) (self-reported).
- On the authors' own 100-image set (25 each of meme, recipe, ad, poem) judged by human evaluators, MiniGPT-4 satisfies 65 requests against 5 for BLIP-2; it explains 8 of 25 memes correctly against 0 (self-reported).
- ChatGPT-judged coverage of COCO ground-truth captions on 5000 images is 66.2% against 27.5% for BLIP-2 (self-reported).
- Ablations on AOK-VQA and GQA: dropping the Q-Former gives 56.9 and 33.4 against 58.2 and 32.2 for the full model; three linear layers lower AOK-VQA to 49.7; also fine-tuning the Q-Former gives 52.1 and 28.0 (self-reported).
- Longer outputs hallucinate more: CHAIRi is 9.6 at 175 words against 7.2 at 28.8 words, and BLIP-2 scores 1.3 at 6.5 words (the unit is not printed) (self-reported).
- On traditional VQA the original model trails BLIP-2 (58.2 against 80.2 on AOK-VQA, 32.2 against 42.4 on GQA); with Vicuna unfrozen through LoRA and VQA data added, the scores become 67.2 and 43.5. BLIP-2 trained on 129M pairs and MiniGPT-4 on 5M (self-reported).

## Limits

- Hallucination in detailed descriptions is unresolved, and spatial understanding is weak.
- Most of the evidence is qualitative, together with one hand-judged set of 100 images.
- The paper does not state the optimizer, learning-rate schedule, precision, Vicuna size or trainable parameter count.
- No seeds or variance.
