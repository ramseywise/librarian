# Self-Reasoning Retrieval-Augmented Model

The Self-Reasoning framework (arXiv 2407.19813, preprint) has the reader produce a trajectory in one pass: a relevance-aware process (RAP) judging whether the documents help, an evidence-aware selective process (EAP) quoting snippets with citations, and a trajectory analysis process (TAP) that reasons over them and gives a short answer. LLaMA2 7B and 13B are fine-tuned in stages on 2,000 filtered trajectories drawn from 10,000 generated by GPT-4.

## Results (self-reported)

- 13B versus Self-RAG: FEVER 83.9 against 72.1; NQ 41.4 against 38.8; PopQA 57.3 against 55.8; ASQA EM recall 35.2 against 31.7.
- ASQA citation recall 72.3 against 68.5 for GPT-4. GPT-4 still leads on NQ with 46.6, on PopQA with 62.5 and on FEVER with 87.7.
- Removing RAP drops FEVER from 83.9 to 72.2.
- Self-RAG needs 46,000 critic instances, against 2,000 here.

## Limits

- Internal inconsistency: hyperparameters say greedy decoding while also listing temperature 0.2. The latency unit is missing, and the noise-robustness result appears only in a figure.
- Wikipedia QA and FEVER only. Trajectory quality depends on the teacher model, and optimizer details are not stated.
