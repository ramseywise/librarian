# RAGChecker

RAGChecker (Ru et al., arXiv 2408.08067, 2024) scores a retrieval-augmented system by checking claims: it extracts claims from the model response and from a ground-truth long-form answer, then tests each against the other text, and against the retrieved chunks, with an entailment checker. Llama3-70B served as extractor and checker.

## Metrics

- **Overall:** precision, recall and F1 of response claims against the reference.
- **Retriever:** claim recall (reference claims found in the retrieved chunks) and context precision (relevant chunks over k, where a chunk is relevant if it entails any reference claim).
- **Generator:** faithfulness, relevant-noise sensitivity, irrelevant-noise sensitivity, hallucination, self-knowledge and context utilisation.

The benchmark is 4,162 queries over 10 domains repurposed from ClapNQ, NovelQA, KIWI and seven RobustQA domains; short answers in seven were rewritten as long-form by GPT-4 and filtered with RefChecker.

## Results (self-reported)

- **Meta-evaluation.** On 280 response pairs (10 domains by 28 pairs from 8 systems, two annotators each), RAGChecker's Pearson correlation with human pairwise preference was 49.66 (correctness), 60.67 (completeness) and 61.93 (overall). RAGAS Answer Similarity scored 41.07, 53.16 and 48.31; human-to-human agreement was 63.67, 71.91 and 70.09. Annotators were within one point on 90.95% of pairs. RAGAS Faithfulness correlated 8.22, 4.90 and 7.83, though the human labels judged correctness and completeness, not groundedness.
- **Retriever swap.** Moving BM25 to E5-Mistral under GPT-4 (300-token chunks, top-20, 10 datasets averaged) raised F1 from 50.3 to 52.7, claim recall from 74.0 to 83.5 and faithfulness from 87.9 to 92.9, and relevant-noise sensitivity from 26.2 to 28.9.
- **Noise.** Across 8 systems, noise sensitivity to relevant chunks was 26.2 to 36.5 against 3.5 to 6.1 for irrelevant ones; the authors read this as generators trusting a relevant chunk wholesale.
- **Context size.** On 3 systems and 3 domains, raising k from 5 to 20 lifted claim recall 61.5 to 77.6, faithfulness 88.1 to 92.2 and F1 51.7 to 53.4, while noise sensitivity rose 34.0 to 35.4.
- **Prompting for faithfulness.** Faithfulness rose 92.2 to 93.6 and context utilisation 59.2 to 63.7, with noise sensitivity worsening 35.4 to 38.1.
- **Overlap.** Chunk overlap of 0.0, 0.2 and 0.4 had little effect (claim recall 77.8 to 78.1).

## Limits

No significance test backs "significantly better". On Spearman for completeness, RAGAS Answer Similarity was higher (61.35 versus 58.11). Only the overall metrics were validated against people; the diagnostic ones were not. Annotators were shown GPT-4 critiques, so the human labels depend partly on an LLM judge. Baselines ran on Llama3-70B instead of default backbones, with the best metric per framework picked after the fact, so the numbers do not compare with RAGAS's own figures. Every metric needs a reference long-form answer, many generated by GPT-4. The setting ablations cover 3 systems and 3 domains, with no variances or repeats, and the benchmark is English and text-only. Models are 2024-era.
