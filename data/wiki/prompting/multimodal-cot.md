# Multimodal Chain-of-Thought

Zhang, Zhang, Li et al. (TMLR 2024) ask whether a model under 1B parameters can learn useful chain-of-thought rationales from text and images, and whether separating rationale generation from answering helps. Their working hypothesis is that hallucinated rationales mislead small models and that vision features reduce them.

## Method

Two T5 models with the same architecture are fine-tuned separately. In stage one the input is the question, context, options and ViT patch features, and the model writes a rationale. In stage two that rationale is attached to the text input and the model predicts the answer. Vision features join after the text encoder through single-head attention and a gated fusion, in which a sigmoid gate blends text and image states. Backbones are initialised from FLAN-Alpaca; stage-one targets are human-annotated rationales.

## Results

All numbers are the paper's own (self-reported). Unless stated, ScienceQA test, fine-tuned.

- Large (738M) scores 90.45% average, above the prior published best of 86.54%; Base (223M) scores 85.31%.
- Text-only, FLAN-Alpaca base (200M), one stage: having it write the rationale ahead of the answer lowers accuracy from 81.63 to 69.32. An answer-then-explanation order scores 69.68.
- Text-only two-stage: rationales score RougeL 90.73 but answers reach 78.57, below no-CoT at 81.63. Manual inspection of 50 sampled errors puts hallucinated rationales at 56%.
- Using captions in place of images adds just 0.80 points, from 78.57 to 79.37. With frozen ViT features, two-stage accuracy reaches 85.31 and rationale RougeL 93.46, and 60.7% of hallucination errors in the same 50 cases are fixed.
- Ablations: dropping the two-stage design gives 82.62 (Base) and 84.56 (Large); dropping vision gives 78.57 (Base) and 83.97 (Large).
- By vision encoder: ViT scores 85.31 and CLIP 84.27, while DETR gets 83.16 and ResNet 82.86. By backbone: UnifiedQA reaches 82.55, FLAN-T5 83.19 and FLAN-Alpaca 85.31.
- A-OKVQA multiple-choice validation: 50.57 for Base against 47.86 language-only. Transferred to MMMU without extra training it scores 28.7, level with OpenFlamingo-2 (9B).
- Of 50 wrong answers, 80% are commonsense errors (maps, counting, alphabet), 14% logical.

## Limits

- Stage one needs human-annotated rationales; rationales generated by InstructBLIP or ChatGPT give 87.76 against 90.45.
- The concurrent LLaVA (90.92) beats the Large model in the paper's own table.
- Seeds, optimizer, schedule and decoding are not stated; evaluation is on science QA, with MMMU transfer at 28.7.
