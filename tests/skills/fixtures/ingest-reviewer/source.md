Invented for a test fixture. No such meeting took place.

Tarnfield Logistics asked us to look at duplicate shipment labels coming out of their
dispatch agent. Priya Venkataraman traced it: the agent retried the label tool whenever a
call timed out, even though the first call had usually gone through on the carrier side.

The fix was a key generated by the client for each label request, kept in Redis with a
24 hour expiry. A second call with the same key returns the stored result and does not
call the carrier again.

We also put a circuit breaker around the tool, the pattern from Michael Nygard's book
Release It!, which opens after three failures in a row.

No duplicates were reported in the two weeks after the change.
